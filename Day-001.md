# Day 001 - AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning

## Objective

To understand the relationship between Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL), and how they contribute to modern intelligent systems.

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is the broader field focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, decision-making, and problem-solving.

Examples:

* Virtual assistants
* Recommendation systems
* Autonomous vehicles
* Medical diagnosis systems

## Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn patterns from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed for every task.

Types of Machine Learning:

* Supervised Learning
* Unsupervised Learning
* Reinforcement Learning

Examples:

* Spam detection
* Stock prediction
* Customer segmentation

## Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large datasets.

Popular Deep Learning Architectures:

* ANN (Artificial Neural Networks)
* CNN (Convolutional Neural Networks)
* RNN (Recurrent Neural Networks)
* Transformers

Applications:

* Image Recognition
* Natural Language Processing
* Speech Recognition
* Generative AI

## Relationship

AI
└── Machine Learning
└── Deep Learning

Every Deep Learning model is a Machine Learning model, and every Machine Learning model is part of Artificial Intelligence, but the reverse is not always true.

## Key Insight

The recent growth of AI has been largely driven by advances in Deep Learning, increased computational power (GPUs), and the availability of massive datasets.

## My Understanding

AI is the ultimate goal of creating intelligent systems. Machine Learning provides a way for systems to learn from data, while Deep Learning enables machines to solve highly complex problems by automatically learning hierarchical representations of data.
